# Community Code of Conduct
### Our commitment to respectful engagement

Our community strives to create a supportive and positive environment for all members. To foster respectful and responsible engagement, we created Bart Community Code of Conduct. By following these guidelines, you're building a community where everyone feels comfortable, heard, and supported.

> **Mission statement**
> Our mission is to facilitate and encourage communities to engage in the trade (barter) of goods and services such that it is mutually beneficial to the parties involved, while also strengthening the community as a whole through its participation — opening access to a range of valuable resources, and to a greater number of individuals, than might otherwise be possible.

---

## 1. Respectful and Courteous Behavior

At Bart, we strive to create a welcoming and inclusive community. As a community member, you are responsible for upholding decent and respectful behavior in your interactions with others. We ask that you treat your fellow community members with kindness, empathy, and respect and to ensure that your content is appropriate for all audiences.

**To maintain a positive and productive community environment, please do not post:**
- Illegal content
- Pornographic, sexually explicit, or mature content (including links to such content or platforms)
- Racist, sexist, bigoted, or otherwise discriminatory content
- Shockingly graphic, grotesque, or obscene content
- Solicitation

*Why follow the rules: By committing to respectful and courteous behavior, we can create a vibrant and supportive community that fosters learning, growth, and connection for all members.*

---

## 2. Protecting My Privacy in the Community

As a member of Bart, we ask that you understand that protecting your privacy is your responsibility. Please ensure that the information you share with the community is what you are comfortable with, including your profile information.

**To safeguard your personal information, please:**
- Be mindful of the information you share in public forums
- Use discretion when sharing personal details, such as your email address or phone number
- Take advantage of the privacy settings available to you, and adjust them as needed to control who can see your information

*Why follow the rules: By taking personal responsibility for your privacy, you can confidently participate in the community and ensure that your information is secure. While our team takes security measures to protect the community, you must also play an active role in protecting yourself.*

---

## 3. Intentional Contributions

To make the most of your experience in Bart, we encourage you to be intentional about your contributions. Engage in conversations and groups that align with your interests and participate meaningfully. Also understand that you will most benefit from the community when also contributing to it by answering questions, sharing your experiences, and participating in events.

**To ensure that your contributions are valuable and respected, you should strive to:**
- Take care to include pictures in posts for items available for barter.
- Images should depict the item offered
- Provide helpful and constructive feedback to other members
- Leave thoughtful comments on topics that interest you
- RSVP for and attend events that intrigue you

*Why follow the rules: By being intentional about your involvement and actively contributing to the community, you can learn from others, build meaningful connections, and help others do the same.*

---

## 4. Best Practices for Sharing Content

We're excited to facilitate community engagement and the benefits of barter. This hinges on community member posts that effectively communicate their offers and asks so that others can be made aware.

**To help make sure your contributions are valued and respected, here are some best practices to keep in mind:**
- Be transparent: Describe your item or service honestly, including its condition
- Share with care: Use real photos of the item you are offering, not stock images
- Respect intellectual property: Only offer copyrighted or trademarked items if you have the right to trade them

*Why follow the rules: By following these practices, members can trust that a listing is what it claims to be, making every trade easier for everyone involved.*

---

## 5. Responsible Use of Community Features

Our community includes many features to help community members interact with one another, including private messaging. We ask that members use these tools responsibly and refrain from spamming or sending unsolicited messages to other members.

**To ensure that the community remains a supportive and respectful environment, we consider the following actions to be spam:**
- Using likes, comments, private messaging, or other social features to draw attention to your profile in a disingenuous way
- Misusing community features and functionality for personal gain or in a way that is not in line with their core purpose
- Leaving irrelevant comments to draw attention to your account
- Posting content that explicitly promotes a product or service
- Posting the same question in multiple locations before anyone has the opportunity to reply

*Why follow the rules: By using community features responsibly and avoiding spamming, we can create a positive and supportive environment where members can engage with one another meaningfully.*

---

## 6. Best Practice for Using Private Messages for Community Building

Private messaging can be a powerful tool for community builders to connect and collaborate with one another.

**When using this feature, we ask that members follow these guidelines:**
- Messages must be related to relevant topics
- Personalize your messages. Boilerplate messages can come across as impersonal and may lack the necessary details to connect with others. Be sure to include a brief explanation of why you are reaching out, and any pertinent information about their involvement
- Have a specific reason, partnership, or opportunity in mind when contacting another member. Avoid sending general messages asking if someone is interested in collaborating

*Why follow the rules: By using private messaging responsibly and with a focus on community building, we can foster connections and partnerships that help us all grow and learn.*

---

## 7. Reporting Code of Conduct Violations

We're committed to maintaining a respectful and inclusive community environment for all members. If you see a post or comment that violates our code of conduct, we encourage you to report it.

**To report a violation, please follow these steps:**
1. Click on the 'Report' icon found at the top right of the post or comment in question
2. Provide a brief description of the issue, along with any relevant details or evidence (link to the post in question, screenshot, etc.)
3. We will review the report and take appropriate action, which may include removing the offending content or taking disciplinary action against the member responsible

Please note that Bart reserves the right to remove posts or accounts that are detrimental to the community without warning.

**Where to get more information:** If you have any additional questions or concerns, please check out the README.md on our GitHub or contact Bart's support team for assistance. By working together, we can maintain a safe and supportive community environment for all members.

| Offense | Action |
|---|---|
| 1st | Warning, and the content is removed from public view |
| 2nd | Temporary suspension |
| 3rd | Permanent ban |

> **Zero-tolerance — illegal content**
> Illegal content (for example: stolen goods, prohibited weapons, or other content illegal under applicable law) does not follow the standard ladder above. It results in immediate permanent ban and content removal on the first offense, and may be reported to law enforcement where required.
